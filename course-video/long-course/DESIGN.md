## Style Prompt

Technical keynote: a restrained dark presentation for source-code education. Layouts follow a strict editorial grid with generous negative space, large declarative headings, concise evidence, and diagrams that behave like presentation builds. The tone is analytical and calm, with precise motion and no decorative spectacle.

## Colors

- Canvas: `#0B0E14`
- Raised surface: `#141923`
- Primary text: `#F1F4F8`
- Secondary text: `#A9B2C3`
- Accent blue: `#4C8DFF`
- Success green: `#58C792`
- Failure red: `#FF6B74`

## Typography

- Headline and Chinese body: `Arial, sans-serif` with the renderer's CJK fallback
- Code and technical labels: `"JetBrains Mono", monospace`

## Motion

- Presentation-build rhythm: 0.35-0.75 second entrances using `power3.out`, `expo.out`, and `sine.out`.
- Primary transition: restrained horizontal push with short crossfade.
- Chapter transition: vertical shutter-like reveal using a solid accent panel.
- Ambient motion is limited to slow grid drift, line growth, and a soft accent glow.

## What NOT to Do

- No neon purple/cyan gradients.
- No glassmorphism, floating card grids, or rounded-pill overload.
- No playful bounce, elastic motion, or cinematic glitch effects.
- No tiny code screenshots or paragraphs that require pausing to read.
- No unexplained numbers, error messages, function names, or source identifiers in
  headlines. Establish the audience's expected result before revealing an anomaly.
- No unsupported claims that are not traceable to the local source snapshot.
- No fabricated terminal output or invented tool order.
